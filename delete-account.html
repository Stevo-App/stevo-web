Delete your Stevo account

How to delete your Stevo account, and what is removed and what is kept.

Last updated: October 4, 2026 · This is the deletion section of the Privacy Policy, word for word.

How to delete your account

We keep your information for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account yourself, from inside the app: Profile (tap your picture at the top right), then Delete account. Deleting removes your name, username, photo, bio, phone number, payment handles, push token, friend list, invites and diagnostic records, and stops you signing in. It is immediate and cannot be undone. Records that other people share with you, such as a settled bet, a poker game or a crew post, are kept for them, with your name replaced by "Deleted user". Deletion is blocked while something is still open between you and another person (an unsettled balance, an accepted bet that has not finished, a Face Off with an accepted bet, or a live poker night you are hosting), because the other person is counting on that record. Crash reports already sent to the crash-reporting service carry only your user id and stay there until that service's own retention period ends. You can also email support@stevoapp.com and we will delete the account for you.
