Poker nights

Run your home poker tournament on Stevo

Stevo runs the clock and keeps the score, so the host gets to play. Set the starting stack and the blind levels, invite your crew, and the clock moves through the levels on its own. Every finish goes into your crew's standings. Stevo keeps score. It never holds, moves or pays out money.

Before the night

Pick the date and send it to your crew. Each player taps I'm in, Maybe or Out. Only I'm in holds a seat.

Cap the table if you only have room for nine. Once it fills, new players join a waitlist. If someone drops out, whoever has waited longest moves into the open seat on their own.

Friends who are not on Stevo can still play as guests.

During the night

  • A blind clock that runs itself. It moves through your levels on its own, so nobody watches a timer.
  • Your house rules. Decide whether a player who goes out can come back in, how many times, and through which level.
  • An optional break. Players still in can take one extra stack. Players already out cannot.
  • The finish order, kept for you. Stevo records each place as players go out.
  • An early finish. Running late? The host can close the night on chip counts.

Across the season

A season adds up points across every tournament your crew plays. Finish high and you climb the standings. Overall shows the season so far. By month splits it month by month.

The host picks how points work. Field size gives a win in a bigger game more points. Fixed table sets the points for each place, like 1500, 1250, 1000 and 750. A participation floor can give everyone points just for showing up. Season ties go to the best single finish.

Here is how it adds up. Say ten players start with 10,000 chips each and play ten levels of 10 minutes, like the night in the picture. With field size points, the winner gets 10, second gets 9 and tenth gets 1. If only six play the next week, that winner gets 6. A win in the bigger game counts for more.

Just a game night?

Not every night is a tournament. Pick a plain poker night and Stevo handles the RSVPs and the calendar. It never counts chips.
