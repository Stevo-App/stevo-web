What is in the app

Everything your crew
already bets on.

Three things carry the app today: the lines board, Face Off, and poker nights. Crews and the Settle tab tie them together.

01 — LINES AND BETS
Real lines, without the book's cut

Spreads, totals and moneylines for the games on tonight, refreshed through the day, with a LIVE toggle for games already underway. Friends betting each other do not owe a sportsbook anything, so Stevo strips the margin out and settles at the fair (no-vig) line: two sides priced -110 / -110 become +100 / +100. Nothing on the board? Write a custom bet in your own words, set the stake and the odds multiplier, and pick who takes it.

  • American, decimal or fractional odds, your choice
  • Multi-pick parlays, with picks that cannot both happen blocked
  • Bets on real games settle themselves the moment the game goes final
  • Private by default; share one with a crew when you want the audience
02 — FACE OFF
Say the bet, wherever the game is on

Start a Face Off with one friend and bet them head to head as the game unfolds — at the stadium, on the couch, or a thousand miles apart with the same game on. A new bet opens on a microphone, not a keyboard: say "he hits a home run next at bat, ten dollars at five to one" and Stevo writes the bet, the stake and the odds into a slip you can fix before it goes over. Both phones stay in sync and the header shows who is up. Each of you declares your own result with I won or I lost, and their answer stays hidden until you have given yours, so you report what you saw rather than reacting to them. Matching answers confirm the bet; a mismatch marks it disputed and nothing settles until you sort it out between you. There is no referee and no host override.

  • Say the money and the odds any way you say them out loud: "ten dollars at five to one", "$5 at 10x", "a buck at even money"
  • Set a dollar line and Stevo nudges you to settle once the running score passes it
  • Every bet nets into one number, and settling posts that single amount
  • Broadcast a single bet to two to four friends and let them take it
03 — POKER NIGHTS AND SEASONS
From the RSVP to who owes whom

Cash nights and tournaments. RSVPs with an automatic waitlist when the table fills, a live blind clock, rebuys, add-ons and an end-of-night chop, and a payout split by finish order. When it is over Stevo turns the buy-ins and the payouts into who owes whom and sends them to the Settle tab. Run a season and every tournament feeds one crew-wide standings table.

  • Season points by field size or a fixed points table, with a participation floor
  • Monthly leaderboards are record-only: the crew hands the money over in the room
  • Guests without a Stevo account can play; you settle their cash in person
Crews

A private group with a 6-character invite code (no I, O, 0 or 1, so it still works read out loud). Everyone in a crew can see and take each other's shared bets, gets invited to its poker nights, and can start a Face Off. Public crews let anyone with the code join; in a private crew only the owner and admins send invites.

Settle

Every bet with the same person totals into one balance under I owe and Owed to me. Tap Pay and Stevo opens Venmo or Cash App addressed to your friend, shows their Zelle details, or just records that you handed over cash. You mark it sent, they confirm received, and the balance clears for both of you.

The money

Stevo never touches it

Stevo is a scorekeeper for friendly bets and games among friends. It is not a sportsbook, a wallet, or a payment processor.

No wallet, no balances, no house
There are no deposits and nothing to cash out. Stevo records amounts; it never holds, moves, charges or pays out a cent.
Settling is a hand-off
Pay through Venmo, Cash App, Zelle or in cash, between the two of you. Stevo opens the other app and then records that you said you sent it.
No rake, no vig
The sportsbook margin is stripped out of every line taken off the board, and Stevo takes no cut of anything.

Stevo is for adults 18 and over. Bet tracking is for entertainment purposes only. More detail is in the FAQ, and the full terms are under Terms.
