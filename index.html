What is in the app

Everything your crew
already bets on.

Three things carry the app today: bets with your crew, Face Off, and poker nights. Crews and friends tie them together.

01 · BETS WITH YOUR CREW
Make the call. Keep the record.

Pick a game on tonight's board, pro or college, or write a bet in your own words and choose who takes it. Your friend accepts before it counts. Bets on real games close themselves off the final score. Everything else closes when both of you report the same result, so the record is one you both agreed to.

  • Bets on real games close on the final score, with nobody keeping track by hand
  • A bet in your own words: both sides report, and matching answers confirm it
  • A live view for games already underway
  • Private by default. Share one with a crew when you want the audience

02 · FACE OFF
Say the bet, wherever the game is on

Start a Face Off with one friend and go head to head as the game unfolds. At the stadium, on the couch, or a thousand miles apart with the same game on. A new bet opens on a microphone, not a keyboard: say "he strikes out this at bat" and Stevo writes it into a slip you can fix before it goes over. Your friend gets it as a proposal and taps Accept before it counts; a decline just drops it. Both phones stay in sync and the header shows who is up.

Each of you declares your own result with I won or I lost, and their answer stays hidden until you have given yours, so you report what you saw rather than reacting to them. Matching answers confirm the bet; a mismatch marks it disputed until you sort it out between you.

  • Say it the way you would say it out loud, then fix the slip before you send it
  • One running score for the whole Face Off, on both phones
  • There is no referee and no host override
  • Broadcast a single bet to two to four friends and let one of them take it
03 · POKER NIGHTS AND SEASONS
From the RSVP to the final table

Schedule the night and the crew answers I'm in, Maybe or Out, with an automatic waitlist when the table fills. On the night Stevo runs the blind clock and tracks who is still in. When it is over, the finish order goes on the record and feeds one standings table for the whole crew. Run a season and every tournament counts toward it.

  • Season points by field size or a fixed points table, with a participation floor
  • Monthly leaderboards alongside the season race
  • Guests without a Stevo account can still take a seat
Crews

A private group with a 6-character invite code (no I, O, 0 or 1, so it still works read out loud). Everyone in a crew can see and take each other's shared bets, gets invited to its poker nights, and can start a Face Off. Public crews let anyone with the code join; in a private crew the code files a request the owner or an admin approves.

Friends

Find your people by username or by name, or text them an invite that gets them the app. Match names from Contacts puts their real names next to their handles, on the phone itself.

Private by design

There is no public feed in Stevo. Your record stays between you and the people in it, Face ID locks the app, and report and block are built in.
